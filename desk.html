The Alignment Desk

A writing accelerator for AI safety

  • In person, Cambridge
  • Saturdays at Meridian
  • Free to attend
Apply RSVP to the apply-athon

Applications close at 12pm
on Sunday 18 October.

The Alignment Desk is a structured writing programme for people in Cambridge or London who want to develop their thinking on AI safety and share it through published writing.

A small cohort meets on Saturdays at Meridian in Cambridge. You'll work on your own projects, with feedback from the group and weekly goals to help you finish them. We expect each participant to publish three pieces during term.

What this is

Dedicated writing time
Weekly Saturday sessions at the Meridian Office. Quiet, structured, and distraction-light. Whiteboards are available in separate rooms for collaboration.
Accountability
You commit to a project at the start of term and report progress each week. The cohort is small, and progress is visible.
Fast feedback
During breaks, participants can sanity-check arguments, get quick red-teams, or talk through ideas that are stuck.
Access to the ecosystem
Where useful, we can help connect participants with researchers, policy professionals, or others in the Cambridge AI safety community. We will also invite relevant experts into the office to give feedback on drafts.
Publishing your work
We expect everyone to publish three pieces during term. If you do not meet this requirement, we will ask you to leave the programme.

Why writing?

Writing forces you to confront gaps in your understanding that remain hidden when you are just reading or thinking. It is also how people in this community come to know your work.

For many aspiring writers, the biggest challenge is turning ideas into finished pieces. Developing a habit of regular output is often what makes the difference between wanting to contribute and doing so.

"Writing is the single biggest difference-maker between reading a lot and efficiently developing real views on important topics."

Holden Karnofsky, Learning By Writing

Who this is for

Location
You're based in Cambridge or London and can attend our Saturday sessions at Meridian in Cambridge.
Preparation
Strong applicants will usually have completed our Alignment Fellowship or a similar programme. Independent reading of core AI safety material, such as BlueDot's AGI Strategy curriculum, is also good preparation.
What we hope you'll leave with
By the end of term, you should have published pieces that show how you think about AI safety and that you can use as work samples when applying for jobs.

What kind of projects?

We are flexible on format. What matters is that you have a concrete output in mind.

  • Literature review

    Review existing work on a technical or governance topic.

  • Explainer or tutorial

    Explain a topic through an interactive piece or tutorial.

  • Research critique

    Steelman or red-team a research agenda.

  • Original argument

    Develop an argument, research note or proposal.

  • Distillation

    Present existing work for a new audience.

  • Career reflection

    Write publicly about your career plans or uncertainties.

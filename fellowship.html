The Alignment Fellowship

A five-week introduction to AI safety for students and professionals in Cambridge, with technical and governance tracks.

  • In person, Cambridge
  • 3–5 hours a week
  • Free to attend

The programme

Weekly readings and discussions

The fellowship introduces the arguments for taking AI risk seriously and the work being done to address it. Each week, you'll meet with a small group to read and discuss the material, following either the technical or governance track.

The fellowship is for undergraduate and postgraduate students and professionals based in Cambridge who are considering AI safety as a possible career.

Two tracks

Technical track

Best suited to people with a background in computer science, mathematics, physics or other quantitative fields.

What you'll cover

  • AI timelines and stakes How quickly AI capabilities may advance and why alignment matters
  • Training modern LLMs How do we train modern LLMs to follow instructions? Does this give them goals, and why might they fail to follow instructions?
  • Interpretability How can we understand what happens inside a model?
  • Evaluating system behaviour How do we test models for reliability and deception?
  • Keeping systems under control Approaches to oversight, monitoring, and safe deployment

Governance track

Best suited to people interested in policy, international relations, law or the political economy of technology.

What you'll cover

  • Technical foundations A non-technical introduction to modern AI systems
  • AI risks and timelines Why safety is important and how timelines affect policy choices
  • AI policy in the US, China and EU How different regions regulate and shape AI development
  • Compute governance The role of chips, computing infrastructure and access controls
  • Responsible scaling policies How AI labs approach safety commitments as capabilities advance
  • Geopolitical strategy International coordination and strategic competition

Support for your next steps

Career coaching

You'll get one-on-one coaching to help you work out your next steps in AI safety.

London visits

We organise trips to London for AI safety socials and office visits, including the London Initiative for Safe AI (LISA).

Hiring referrals

You'll join our referral database, which we use to connect fellows with people looking to hire in AI safety.

Compute for experiments

We want technical fellows to tackle these problems head on, so we'll give you compute to run your own experiments.

From past fellows

What we expect from you

We invest significant time and resources into each cohort, including one-on-one career coaching and support for your next steps. In return, we ask that you invest too.

Do the readings

Each week has core readings and optional deeper dives. Come ready to work through the core readings during the session.

Join the discussions

Attend the weekly sessions and take an active part in the discussion. We want you to ask questions and share your thinking.

Engage seriously

We're looking for people who see AI safety as a plausible career option. The fellowship is a chance to explore whether it could be a good fit for you and how you might contribute.

FAQ

No prior AI safety experience is required. Choose the technical or governance track based on your background and interests.

No, there are no fees associated with the Alignment Fellowship.

Yes. The Alignment Fellowship is hosted in-person in Cambridge, UK. We expect almost all applicants to already be based in either Cambridge or London.

For London-based participants, we may be able to cover travel expenses from London to Cambridge if it would otherwise prohibit you from joining the programme.

We expect participants to commit roughly 3–5 hours per week to readings, discussions and assignments.

When applying, we will ask you for your availability. Weekly meeting times will be finalised in the weeks leading up to the first session. We will choose times that maximise the number of participants able to attend.
